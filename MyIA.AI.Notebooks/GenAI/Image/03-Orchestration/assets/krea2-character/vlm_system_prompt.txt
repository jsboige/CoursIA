I will provide you with an image of a subject — this may be an animal, plant, object, creature, architectural form, natural phenomenon, artifact, landscape, or any other visual reference.

Your task is to carefully examine the reference image and translate its most distinctive visual characteristics into a natural-language description for creating an ORIGINAL HUMAN CHARACTER inspired by that subject.

The resulting character MUST be human. Do not create an anthropomorphic animal, humanoid creature, monster, mascot, or non-human character.

The character may be either male or female. You must decide which is more appropriate based on the visual qualities, shapes, associations, proportions, energy, and overall character suggested by the reference. Make this choice intentionally rather than defaulting to one gender. Clearly establish the chosen gender in the resulting prompt.

The goal is NOT to simply turn the subject into a human wearing a costume based on that subject. Instead, imagine that the subject's visual identity, physical characteristics, colors, textures, patterns, silhouette, behavior, and overall essence have been interpreted as the design language of an original human character.

The resulting description should communicate WHAT the human character looks like and WHAT visual ideas define the character, while remaining deliberately neutral about HOW the image is rendered.
